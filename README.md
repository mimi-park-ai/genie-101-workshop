# Genie 101: Data to Agents in a Day

A beginner-friendly, hands-on Databricks workshop for university students. It's adapted from the
**Genie Zero to Hero (Customer Service Operations)** workshop, rebuilt for people who have never
used Databricks, and for **Databricks Free Edition**: every student signs up for their own free
account on the day.

> Everything here is **100% synthetic**: the bank, its customers, and all the data are made up.
> `labs/` is written for students; `instructor/` is for facilitators.

## The story

You join **Summit Financial**, a fictional retail bank with about 1,000 customers. Its Customer
Service team exports its data to **Google Sheets**, and the VP of Customer Service has questions:

- *"What's our NPS, and which channel is dragging it down?"*
- *"Which open complaints are critical, and who are they for?"*
- *"Are we breaching our 8-week complaint deadline?"*

Over the day you turn those spreadsheets into clean data, a dashboard, and an AI agent, then
teach the agent your business, put it on a schedule, and govern it.

## Agenda (about 5 hours)

| # | Lab | Time | You'll learn |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Databricks 101](labs/01_databricks_101.md) | 30 min | Sign up for Free Edition; tour the workspace |
| 2 | [Meet the data and upload it](labs/02_upload_google_sheets.md) | 20 min | Understand the five datasets; turn spreadsheets into tables |
| 3 | [Visual Data Prep → gold tables](labs/03_visual_data_prep.md) | 40 min | Clean and join data with no code |
| 4 | [AI/BI Dashboard](labs/04_dashboard.md) | 35 min | Build a dashboard, with AI helping |
| 5 | [Build a Genie Agent](labs/05_genie_agent.md) | 35 min | Create the Complaint-Triage agent; benchmark it |
| 6 | [Semantic layer](labs/06_semantic_layer.md) | 25 min | Describe your data so AI understands it |
| 7 | [Metric views: before & after](labs/07_metric_views.md) | 35 min | Define metrics once; measure the improvement |
| 8 | [Scheduling](labs/08_scheduling.md) | 20 min | Refresh data and send reports automatically |
| 9 | [Agent governance](labs/09_agent_governance.md) | 25 min | Control who sees what; prove the agent is right |
| 10 | [AI Gateway](labs/10_ai_gateway.md) | 20 min | Rate-limit and monitor AI usage |

Add about 45 minutes for lunch and two short breaks. Labs 1–7 are the core; 8–11 can be cut for a
shorter session (see [instructor guide](instructor/INSTRUCTOR_GUIDE.md)).

## The data

Five Google Sheets, generated by [`scripts/generate_data.py`](scripts/generate_data.py) and
saved as CSVs in [`data/`](data/):

| Sheet | Rows | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `customers` | ~1,000 | Customers and their segment (Retail / Premier / Wealth) |
| `service_cases` | ~3,000 | Complaints and service requests, with an 8-week (56-day) deadline |
| `interactions` | 4,000 | Calls, app, web, and branch contacts |
| `nps_survey` | 800 | Customer satisfaction survey scores (0–10) |
| `call_insights` | ~380 | AI-written summaries of complaint conversations (calls, chats, branch visits) |

The raw sheets are deliberately a little messy (inconsistent capitalization, stray spaces,
duplicate rows, blanks) so the data-prep lab has real work to do. Everything is **100% synthetic**.

Regenerate before each workshop so dates line up with the day:

```bash
python3 scripts/generate_data.py --as-of 2026-12-03
```

## Before running it with students

Do the [Free Edition dry run](instructor/FREE_EDITION_DRY_RUN.md) on a brand-new Free Edition
account. Several features used here (AI Gateway, MCP, parts of the semantic layer, Genie scheduled
tasks) are new or in preview, and Free Edition support for them was not confirmed when this was
written.
